# Privacy Notice Template

> This is a starting point for the operator of a public Papercut deployment. Review it with the laws and hosting setup that apply to you before publishing it as a legal privacy policy.

## What Papercut Processes

Papercut processes selected images and PDF files in the user's browser. The file bytes are not sent to a Papercut conversion server.

## What the Host May Receive

The hosting provider may receive ordinary web requests, IP addresses, user-agent data, timestamps, and error logs according to its own privacy policy. Configure retention and analytics deliberately and document them here before launch.

## Local Browser Data

Papercut may keep temporary object URLs and in-memory state while the page is open. Users can remove files from the workspace or close the tab. Do not claim persistent local storage unless it is implemented and documented.

## Third Parties

List the hosting provider, analytics providers, error monitoring providers, fonts, CDNs, and any other third party that receives requests. Do not add a provider without updating this notice and reviewing whether it can access document content or filenames.
